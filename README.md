# Gap Geometry

Exploring the geometry beneath information.

---

This is the public home for the Gap Geometry framework — independent research on the algebraic and geometric structure of a constant that recurs across published mathematics and physics, and the irreducible distance between that constant and unity.

Three principles run through the work: every claim is verifiable at stated precision, every classification is honest about its evidence level, and every change is documented in plain view. There is no editorial gate other than the math itself.
